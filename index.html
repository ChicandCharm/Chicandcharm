Our Story

Started together, one piece at a time

Chic & Charm was started by sisters-in-law who wanted more than just another closet of clothes — they wanted pieces that felt special. What began as a shared idea between them is still just the beginning.

Every top and co-ord set in this collection was found one by one — through trips to Delhi, Mumbai, and China — hand-picked rather than mass-ordered, because that's how we'd want to shop for ourselves too. We're based in Ludhiana.
