Our Story

Started together, one piece at a time

Chic & Charm was started by sisters-in-law who wanted more than just another closet of clothes — they wanted pieces that felt special. What began as a shared idea between them is still just the beginning.

Every top and co-ord set in this collection was found one by one — through trips to Delhi, Mumbai, and China — hand-picked rather than mass-ordered, because that's how we'd want to shop for ourselves too. We're based in Ludhiana.

Policies

Return & Exchange Policy

At Chic & Charm, we want you to love what you order. If something isn't right, here's how we can help.

Return & Exchange Window

You can request a return or exchange within 30 days of the delivery date.

Eligibility

To be eligible, the item must be unused and unwashed (tried on only for fit and sizing), with all original tags and packaging intact. Exchanges for a different size are welcome as long as the item meets this condition. Items that are damaged, defective, or not what you ordered are also covered.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Message us on WhatsApp or email within 30 days of delivery with your order details and, if there's a damage or defect, a clear photo of the issue. We'll arrange a pickup where available, or share instructions for sending the item back to us.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll process your exchange or refund within 7-10 working days. We do not accept returns or exchanges on used or altered products.

Policies

Terms & Conditions

By ordering from Chic & Charm, you agree to the following terms.

Orders & Availability

All orders are subject to availability. Since many of our pieces are sourced in limited quantities, an item may sell out before your order is confirmed — in that case, we'll let you know and offer an alternative or a full refund.

Pricing

All prices are listed in INR and exclude shipping unless stated otherwise. Prices are subject to change without prior notice.

Product Variation

Since several of our pieces feature hand embroidery and natural fabrics, slight variation in color, print placement, or embroidery detail between the photo and the product you receive is normal and not considered a defect. Screen settings may also affect how colors appear.

Contact

Questions about an order or these terms can be sent to us anytime via WhatsApp, call, or email — see our Contact section below.
